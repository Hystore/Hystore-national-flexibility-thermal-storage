# Repository Governance - HYSTORE Project

This repository is part of the **HYSTORE Open Source Project**, funded by the European Union’s Horizon Europe programme. It follows a distributed development model overseen by a central Technical Steering Committee.

## 👥 Roles and Responsibilities

### 1. Repository Maintainers
The day-to-day management of **this specific repository** is handled by its designated **Maintainers**.
- **Current Lead Partner:** KTH
- **Role:** Maintainers are responsible for technical oversight, code quality, and reviewing Pull Requests (PRs).
- **Scope:** They have the authority to merge technical fixes and minor updates that align with the established WP4 technical specifications.

### 2. The Technical Steering Committee (TSC)
Strategic decisions and major architectural changes that affect the interoperability of HYSTORE are governed by the **TSC**.
- **Composition:** Representatives from the core technical partners (ARC, CNR, R2M, and KTH).
- **Supervision:** Any change proposal that modifies the core logic, the IoT-based Ontology integration, or the project’s strategic roadmap must be escalated by the Maintainers to the TSC for formal approval.

## 🗳 Decision-Making Process

1. **Local Review:** Pull Requests are first reviewed by this repository's Maintainers.
2. **TSC Escalation:** If a change is significant, the Maintainer submits a proposal to the TSC.
3. **Consensus & Final Word:** Decisions within the TSC are made by consensus. In the event of a disagreement, the **Project Coordinator (ARC)** holds the final decision-making authority to ensure strict compliance with the Grant Agreement (GA 101096789).

## 🌍 Sustainability
This governance structure ensures that this specific component remains stable and interoperable throughout the project's lifecycle (M1-M48) and provides a clear framework for its long-term maintenance after the official funding period ends.
